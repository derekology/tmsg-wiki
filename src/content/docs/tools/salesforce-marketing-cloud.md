Salesforce Marketing Cloud Engagement is Salesforce's marketing platform for building and operating customer communications and journeys. Salesforce documentation describes tools for journeys and automations, including workflows that use contact data, events, and channel activities. Product capabilities, packaging, and connections to other Salesforce products vary by edition and configuration.

## Where it fits

Teams use Marketing Cloud Engagement to manage marketing audiences and execute communications such as email and mobile messaging, often through automated journeys. A journey can coordinate messages and decision points over time, but its quality depends on reliable entry data, explicit exit and suppression rules, consent controls, and well-defined ownership of customer records.

Organizations integrating it with Salesforce CRM should document how subscriber and contact data relate to CRM records, how updates synchronize, and which system owns preferences and suppression. Confirm the actual integration and data model in the target account rather than assuming that products sharing the Salesforce name use identical data structures.

## Engagement and Marketing Cloud Next

Marketing Cloud Engagement is distinct from [Salesforce Marketing Cloud Next](/tools/salesforce-marketing-cloud-next/), a newer Salesforce marketing offering built on the Salesforce platform. The names can be confusing; evaluate each product's architecture, feature availability, migration path, and fit for the organization's current Salesforce environment directly with Salesforce.

## Evaluation questions

Assess journey complexity, volume, data model, integrations, deliverability operations, consent management, reporting, administration effort, and total cost. Test failure, re-entry, duplicate-event, and unsubscribe scenarios before activating automated communications.
