Mailchimp is a marketing platform whose help center covers email campaigns, audiences, automation, landing pages, integrations, reports, and email delivery. It is commonly used to manage permission-based contact audiences and communicate with them through email, though product offerings can extend beyond that core.

## Typical workflows

A team may collect signups, organize contacts into audiences or segments, send a newsletter or campaign, and review engagement reports. Automation can handle repeatable messages such as a welcome sequence. Good results depend on valid permission, accurate contact data, appropriate frequency, and clear suppression rules—not only on the sending platform.

Keep the contact model and consent history understandable. Review how imports, unsubscribes, bounces, and duplicates affect active audiences. If Mailchimp is connected to a shop, CRM, or analytics tool, verify which system owns customer details and how updates are reconciled.

## Compare by job to be done

Mailchimp can overlap with [HubSpot](/tools/hubspot/), [Klaviyo](/tools/klaviyo/), and [Adobe Marketo Engage](/tools/marketo-engage/), but the overlap varies by feature and plan. Compare automation complexity, integrations, audience management, reporting, permissions, deliverability controls, and cost for your use case. Check current pricing and feature limits directly with the vendor.
