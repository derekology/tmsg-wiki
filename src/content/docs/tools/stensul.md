Stensul is an email creation platform for building and reviewing campaign emails in a shared workspace. Its product information describes a modular no-code builder, reusable components, collaboration and approval workflows, and connections to marketing automation and asset-management systems.

## Where it fits

Email teams can use Stensul to create and review messages before sending them through a connected marketing platform. Stensul lists integrations for delivering approved emails to systems including Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Journey Optimizer, Marketo, and Oracle Eloqua. This positions it in the email production workflow; the connected system may still handle audience execution and sending.

## Evaluation considerations

Build and review a representative email using Stensul's reusable components, then send it to the intended marketing platform through the proposed integration. Check whether dynamic content, links, assets, and rendering survive the handoff, and which edits or approvals happen in each system.

Compare the effort of creating and maintaining shared templates and components with the existing production process. Confirm that the integration supports the specific destination and handoff workflow; Stensul's authoring and approval role is separate from audience selection and sending.
