Stensul is an email creation platform for building and reviewing campaign emails in a shared workspace. Its product information describes a modular no-code builder, reusable components, collaboration and approval workflows, and connections to marketing automation and asset-management systems.

## Where it fits

Email teams can use Stensul to create and review messages before sending them through a connected marketing platform. Stensul lists integrations for delivering approved emails to systems including Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Journey Optimizer, Marketo, and Oracle Eloqua. This positions it in the email production workflow; the connected system may still handle audience execution and sending.

The main operational dependencies are reusable templates, clear ownership of content and approvals, and a tested handoff to the sending platform. Teams should confirm that the integration supports their chosen workflow and verify that dynamic content, links, and rendering behave as expected after handoff. Workflow controls can support brand and review processes, but they do not replace an organization's own governance.

## Evaluation questions

Assess how Stensul fits with the existing email service or marketing automation platform, template and asset processes, approval requirements, and quality-assurance steps. Compare the time and effort needed to maintain shared components against the current production workflow, and confirm current integrations with the vendor.
