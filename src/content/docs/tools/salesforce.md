Salesforce is a CRM platform used to manage customer and prospect relationships across business functions. Salesforce describes a product portfolio spanning sales, service, marketing, commerce, and related data and integration capabilities. For marketing teams, the relevant question is often how CRM records, campaign engagement, sales activity, and downstream customer outcomes are represented and connected.

## Where it fits

A CRM is not just an address book. Its record model and processes can support account and contact management, lead follow-up, opportunity tracking, and coordination with marketing systems. Salesforce may be the system of record for some of those objects while other platforms handle campaign execution, analytics, or data movement.

Map ownership before implementation: decide which system creates each record, which fields are authoritative, how changes synchronize, and how teams handle duplicates and lifecycle transitions. Document permissions and reporting definitions as carefully as the integration diagram.

## How to compare it

Salesforce overlaps with [HubSpot](/tools/hubspot/) in CRM and marketing workflows and can be connected to dedicated automation products such as [Adobe Marketo Engage](/tools/marketo-engage/). A useful evaluation compares the required data model, process flexibility, administration effort, integrations, reporting, user roles, and total operating cost. There is no universal best fit; an expansive platform can be a poor match if the organization cannot govern and maintain it.
