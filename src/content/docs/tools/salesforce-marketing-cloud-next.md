Salesforce Marketing Cloud Next is Salesforce's newer marketing offering, announced in 2025 and positioned as built on the Salesforce platform. Salesforce describes it as combining marketing workflows with Salesforce data, applications, and AI-assisted or agentic capabilities. Exact functions, editions, and availability depend on the customer's Salesforce environment and current product packaging.

## How it differs from Marketing Cloud Engagement

Marketing Cloud Next should not be assumed to be a simple label change or automatic replacement for [Marketing Cloud Engagement](/tools/salesforce-marketing-cloud/). The products have different platform foundations and may have different data models, implementation paths, and feature sets. Ask Salesforce to identify which product and edition a proposal covers, which features are available now, and what a migration would require.

Salesforce's positioning emphasizes connected data and AI-assisted campaign work. For an evaluation, translate those claims into concrete use cases: which data is available to a marketer, what the system can generate or execute, which actions require approval, and how teams review access, decisions, and outcomes.

## Evaluation questions

Map required channels and journeys, CRM and data dependencies, existing automations, security and permission boundaries, consent and suppression behavior, reporting needs, and migration costs. Confirm product availability and licensing in the target region and org with Salesforce documentation and the account team; public announcements do not guarantee that every feature is available in every configuration.
