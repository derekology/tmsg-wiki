Klaviyo is a marketing and service platform focused on customer data and messaging. Its product information and help center cover email, SMS, campaigns, automated flows, analytics, integrations, and customer data capabilities. The available channels and features depend on product configuration and current plan.

## Common marketing use

For a commerce or subscription business, a lifecycle program might respond to events such as signup, purchase, or a change in customer activity. A marketer can use audience rules and flows to coordinate timely messages, while analytics help evaluate engagement and outcomes. The event quality, consent status, suppression rules, and frequency limits determine whether those workflows are useful and appropriate.

Start with a small number of valuable journeys and explicit entry and exit conditions. Test how profile updates, unsubscribes, and repeated events behave before activating a flow. Keep the source of truth for customer and order data clear, especially when integrating with a commerce platform or CRM.

## Comparing platforms

Klaviyo overlaps with broader marketing automation platforms such as [HubSpot](/tools/hubspot/) and [Adobe Marketo Engage](/tools/marketo-engage/), and with email-focused services such as [Mailchimp](/tools/mailchimp/). Compare data integrations, channel needs, segmentation, automation, reporting, deliverability tooling, consent handling, and total cost for the actual volume and use case.
