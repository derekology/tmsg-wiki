Treasure AI Studio is an AI-assisted chat interface for working with a Treasure AI account. Its documentation describes querying data, building customer audiences, orchestrating journeys, and generating reports from web, desktop, and mobile applications.

## Where it fits

Studio can help marketing, analytics, and data teams work with customer data and CDP resources through conversation rather than navigating every task manually in the platform console. The documented capabilities include generating and running queries, creating segments, working with journeys and workflows, and producing reports or files. Treasure AI says that tool calls and actions are visible as they run; outputs and changes should still be reviewed according to the organization's operating process.

Studio requires a Treasure AI account and AI Credits provisioned for that account. Feature availability varies by platform, and channel or data capabilities depend on the entitlements of the connected account and its configuration.

## Evaluation considerations

Identify which users can query data or create and change CDP resources, what approval or review is needed before a journey or activation is launched, and how generated queries, files, and recommendations are validated. Review the vendor's security, access-control, data-retention, and regional-processing documentation with the teams responsible for privacy and platform governance.

Consider whether a conversational interface complements the existing console and workflows, which platforms users need, and how AI Credit usage will be monitored. Treat claims about time savings and AI-generated results as vendor positioning; validate them with representative tasks and review criteria.
