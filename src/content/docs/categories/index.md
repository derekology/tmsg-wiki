Categories group services by their primary use, not by their agent-readiness level. A service's capabilities and constraints belong in its article.

## Browse by category

- [Communication](/categories/communication/): services for conversations, messaging, and shared discussion.
- [Developer tools](/categories/developer-tools/): services supporting software development, repositories, and issue tracking.
- [Knowledge management](/categories/knowledge-management/): services for organizing pages, project context, and structured knowledge.

## Other ways to browse

See the alphabetical [apps and services directory](/apps/) or learn [how assessments work](/assessment/). The [contribution guide](/contributing/) explains how to add an article and its category links.
