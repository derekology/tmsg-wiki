---
title: Product-Led Growth
description: How product use can drive acquisition, activation, expansion, and retention.
---

Product-led growth (PLG) is a go-to-market strategy in which product experience is a primary driver of customer acquisition, expansion, or retention. People can learn about value through using the product, rather than relying only on a sales presentation or advertising. PLG is a strategy, not a pricing model: free trials and freemium offers can support it, but neither guarantees it.

## What a PLG system needs

- **A clear first-value moment:** A new user should be able to reach a useful outcome without unnecessary setup.
- **A guided product journey:** Onboarding, help, and prompts should support the job the user came to do.
- **Responsible product signals:** Usage patterns can help a team understand adoption or identify when a user might benefit from assistance. Signals need context; activity is not automatically buying intent.
- **A path to human help or a larger contract:** Sales and customer success can support complex setup, security review, expansion, or organization-wide adoption.

Product and marketing teams should agree on activation, engaged use, and retention definitions. Instrument the relevant actions and use those data to improve the experience, not just to trigger more messages.

## PLG and sales-led growth

These strategies are not opposites. A product can create initial understanding and usage, while sales teams support evaluation, procurement, implementation, or expansion. Salesforce describes organizations combining product usage signals with sales engagement. The appropriate balance depends on product complexity, buying process, and customer support needs.

## What to measure

Track time to first value, activation by cohort, use of key capabilities, conversion between product and commercial stages, and retention. Break results down by customer segment so strong averages do not hide poor experiences for a meaningful group.
