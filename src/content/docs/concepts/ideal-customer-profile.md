---
title: Ideal Customer Profile
description: Define the organizations or customers most likely to succeed with your offer.
---

An ideal customer profile (ICP) describes the kind of customer for whom an offer is a strong fit and who is likely to receive meaningful value from it. In B2B marketing, an ICP usually describes an organization or account; a **buyer persona** describes a person or role involved in a decision. The two are related but answer different questions.

## Building a usable profile

Ground the profile in existing customer evidence rather than aspiration. Review customers who achieve the intended outcome, renew or continue using the offer, and can be served sustainably. Look for patterns in:

- Firmographics such as industry, organization size, and location
- Needs, use cases, and the costs of the problem being solved
- Existing systems or operating conditions that affect implementation
- Buying process, stakeholders, and constraints
- Outcomes, retention, and service effort

Use observable criteria where possible. A profile based only on broad labels such as “innovative company” is hard to use consistently.

## Put the ICP to work

Use it to prioritize account research, shape messaging, assess channel fit, and identify where sales or product feedback should update assumptions. Pair the profile with disqualifiers and reasons a customer may not be a fit. Revisit it when the product, market, or customer evidence changes.

An ICP is a prioritization tool, not a claim that customers outside the profile cannot succeed. Avoid turning it into a rigid gate without checking the cost of excluding unusual but valuable customers.
