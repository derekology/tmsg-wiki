title: Revenue Operations

Revenue operations (RevOps) is an operating approach for coordinating the people, processes, data, and systems involved in generating and retaining revenue. It commonly connects marketing, sales, and customer success; some organizations also include finance, product, or other functions. The exact scope varies, so a RevOps label alone does not tell you how a company is organized.

## What RevOps works on

RevOps often addresses shared lifecycle definitions, planning and forecasting, lead and account handoffs, process design, CRM and marketing-system governance, reporting, and operational measurement. Its purpose is to reduce conflicting processes and information gaps across teams—not to centralize every decision or replace functional expertise.

For example, marketing and sales can agree what qualifies as a sales-ready lead, who owns follow-up, how quickly a response is expected, and how rejected leads return to nurture. The definition, routing rule, and reporting logic should match across teams and systems.

## A practical starting point

Map the customer's path through marketing, sales, onboarding, and renewal. Identify where ownership changes, what data is required, and which steps create delay or inconsistent reporting. Choose a small number of shared definitions and metrics, assign owners, and review whether the workflow actually works before automating it.

RevOps is broader than sales operations, which focuses on sales-team effectiveness. It is also distinct from buying another CRM or analytics tool: technology supports an operating model but does not create cross-team agreement by itself.
