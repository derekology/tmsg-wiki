---
title: Marketing Data Governance
description: The decision rights, definitions, and controls that guide responsible and consistent use of marketing data.
---

Marketing data governance is the set of responsibilities, decisions, and controls that guide how marketing data is defined, accessed, used, maintained, and retired. It connects day-to-day marketing work with organizational expectations for data quality, privacy, security, and accountability.

Governance is not a single committee or a software feature. It should make clear who can make which decisions, how rules are applied in connected systems, and how issues are raised and resolved. The right structure varies with the organization's size, data, customer relationships, and operating risks.

## Establish ownership and shared definitions

Assign accountable owners for important data and decisions. This may include a business owner for lifecycle definitions, a system owner for access and configuration, and technical owners for integrations. Responsibilities can be shared, but each decision should have a clear route to resolution.

Agree on meanings for fields and events that cross team boundaries. For example, define what lifecycle stages mean, which system is authoritative for a preference, and what evidence counts as a conversion. Document the meaning, allowed values, source, and change process so that systems and reports do not silently interpret the same term differently.

## Put rules into operating practice

Policies are useful only when reflected in processes and systems. Depending on context, governance may include:

- Access based on role and need, with review when responsibilities change.
- Data quality checks and a process to correct known errors.
- Clear purposes and limits for collecting, sharing, and activating information.
- Handling for preferences, consent records, retention periods, and deletion requests.
- Review of new integrations, vendors, audiences, and use cases.
- Documentation, monitoring, escalation, and recovery for important workflows.

The specifics depend on organizational policy, contracts, and applicable laws. This page is general operational information, not legal advice; privacy and marketing requirements vary by jurisdiction and context.

## Keep governance proportionate and reviewable

Controls should reduce meaningful risk without making ordinary work opaque or impossible to operate. Document who approved a rule, where it is enforced, how exceptions are handled, and when it should be reviewed. Revisit controls when data sources, purposes, systems, or customer journeys change.

Governance is part of the broader [marketing system](/concepts/marketing-systems/), and often intersects with [marketing operations](/concepts/marketing-operations/) and [revenue operations](/concepts/revenue-operations/).
