AgentReady is a wiki about how apps and services can be used by agents. Its documentation-based assessments describe API and MCP capabilities, permissions, and operational constraints without an overall score or readiness tier. Documented support is not evidence of a live-tested integration.

## Browse the wiki

Start with the alphabetical [apps and services directory](/apps/) or browse [developer tools](/categories/developer-tools/), [knowledge management](/categories/knowledge-management/), and [communication](/categories/communication/) in the [category index](/categories/).

## Service assessments

- [GitHub](/apps/github/): issue tracking and repository workflows.
- [Notion](/apps/notion/): workspace pages and project context.
- [Slack](/apps/slack/): discussion retrieval and messaging.

Each entry separates read and write capabilities, cites official documentation, and records its source-review date and untested boundaries.

## How assessments work

The [assessment method](/assessment/) defines per-capability evidence labels, an API/MCP-first approach, and the distinction between documentation review and live testing. Missing evidence means **Not verified**, not unsupported. Permissions, event delivery, pagination, and limits matter as much as the presence of an interface.
