The Marketing Systems Wiki explains how marketing tools, data, and operating practices fit together. Start with a concept, compare a platform, or follow a measurement question. Pages aim to be useful to both people learning the field and practitioners improving an existing system.

## A few useful starting points

- **Planning a marketing system?** Begin with the [ideal customer profile](/concepts/ideal-customer-profile/) and [buyer journey](/concepts/buyer-journey-and-funnel/), then map the tools and handoffs needed.
- **Trying to understand performance?** Review [attribution](/measurement/marketing-attribution/) alongside [customer acquisition cost and lifetime value](/measurement/cac-and-customer-lifetime-value/); each depends on explicit definitions and assumptions.
- **Improving a campaign?** Standardize [UTM parameters](/measurement/utm-parameters/) and test a focused change with [A/B experimentation](/measurement/experimentation-and-ab-testing/).
